The life of a receipt — from checkout to your account

1

Receipt is issued

The store issues a digital receipt to your email or phone number — signed with Ed25519, so it can't be forged or quietly altered.

2

It lands in your account

If you're registered with Valuon under that email or phone, the receipt appears in your account automatically — no QR scanning, no extra steps.

3

Warranty tracks itself

The warranty period is calculated automatically and the receipt stays in your profile — nothing to remember or track by hand.
