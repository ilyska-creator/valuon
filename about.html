About Valuon

Digital receipts you can trust.

Mission

Make every receipt digital, cryptographically secure, and always accessible. No more faded thermal paper, lost warranties, or disputes with the store.

How It Works

The seller issues a receipt with an Ed25519 digital signature. The buyer gets a PDF with a QR code — anyone can scan it to verify the receipt's authenticity. Warranty dates are tracked in your dashboard with one-click calendar export.

Why Valuon

  • Ed25519 cryptographic signature — receipts cannot be forged
  • Automatic warranty calculation and tracking
  • Receipts never fade or get lost — always in the cloud
  • Instant QR verification without registration
  • Free for buyers
