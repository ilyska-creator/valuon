Valuon Roadmap: a unified platform for official digital receipts

Development plan from a working MVP to a unified access platform for official receipts across countries.

Two roles for Valuon, not one universal one

Role A

Primary issuer

Estonia, Ireland, Malta, Finland, Liechtenstein, the US — where receipt fiscalization isn't mandatory, a Valuon receipt can be a genuine official digital receipt, not an imitation of one.

Role B

Registry on top of the official one

Germany, France, Italy, Spain and others — the receipt is already signed by a certified fiscal cash register. Valuon connects to that data and gives the receipt a second life for the buyer.

Role C — deferred

Becoming a certified provider

Becoming a certified fiscal middleware provider ourselves (like fiskaly/efsta) is an expensive, multi-year path. Decided no earlier than 2028, once scale is proven.

Warranty tracking, ownership history and resale work on top of both roles as an add-on layer — they make returning to the app meaningful, but they aren't the goal on their own.

Role A + Role B — foundation

MVP: current stage — what's already built

A working product you can open, sign up for, and start using today. All further stages build on this foundation.

  • Email/password registration, email confirmation, password recovery
  • Bot protection on login and registration
  • Email change, name editing in settings
  • Manual item entry: name, type, brand, price, serial number, purchase date, warranty term
  • Automatic warranty end date calculation
  • Notifications section: warranty ≤30 days or expired
  • Receipt photo/PDF upload, drag&drop, attachment to item
  • Business: store creation, Ed25519 key generation
  • Business: multi-item receipt issuance with digital signature
  • Business: PDF with QR code, sales chart
  • Verification: QR camera scan, PDF upload, Ed25519 check
  • Dark/light theme, RU/EN multilingual support
  • Server-side signature verification: Edge Function with per-IP rate limiting, anonymous verification page
  • Business: revenue, average receipt, period charts, filters, receipt deletion
  • Receipt draft: auto-save of the form and restore
  • Customer autocomplete by email with customer history
  • Business: shop logo upload and display in PDF receipts
  • Warranty calendar export to the user's device
Текущая работа
Infrastructure

Stage 1: strengthen the technical foundation

Before inviting more stores and users — strengthen what's already built.

  • Automated versioning of database schema and access policies
  • Enable password strength check on registration and reset
  • Enhanced browser protection policies and security headers
  • Additional server-side validation for uploaded receipt files
  • Versioning for the cryptographic receipt signature format
  • Snapshot store details at receipt issuance time for verification
  • Unified total calculations and formatting in PDF receipts
  • Additional data integrity rules for receipt line items
  • Shop and receipt currency selection (multicurrency, EUR by default)
  • Estonian interface language (ET)
  • Server-side receipt signing: private key stored only in Edge Function (Vault), never sent to the user's device
  • Bulk historical receipt import via CSV
  • Receipt export (CSV / Excel for accounting)
This stage establishes a solid technical foundation ahead of onboarding more stores and scaling receipt volume.
Месяцы 1–3
Role A + activation

Stage 2: customer activation — D30 retention

Manual receipt entry is the #1 barrier to return. OCR, email import, and push notifications should turn the app into a habit, not a one-time experiment.

  • Auto-fill item fields from receipt photo (OCR)
  • Import receipts from email (Amazon, Ozon, AliExpress, etc.)
  • Push notifications for warranty expiry
  • Actual weekly newsletter delivery (cron / Edge Function)
  • Export warranty end date to calendar (.ics for Google/Apple Calendar)
  • Service history log for each item (repairs, dates, cost)
  • Warranty claim wizard: pre-filled email to seller with receipt link
  • Transfer item to new owner on resale (QR with ownership history)
  • PWA with one-tap camera access
Месяцы 3–6
Role B + business tools

Stage 3: tools for business

Stores won't issue receipts manually. We need POS integrations, an API, and seamless onboarding — only then does receipt issuance become a natural part of the sale.

  • Plugins for POS and e-commerce (Shopify, WooCommerce, Lightspeed)
  • Public REST API for programmatic receipt issuance
  • Webhooks for events (receipt issued, receipt verified)
  • Multi-user store accounts with employee roles
  • Custom receipt and verification page branding
  • Return/exchange/void workflow for issued receipts
  • Store key rotation and revocation
  • Advanced analytics: LTV, average order, warranty claim rate, % of returning customers
  • Extended warranty upsell at receipt issuance time (commission)
Месяцы 6–12
B2B2C infrastructure

Stage 4: monetization and network effects

The product currently has no payment functionality at all. First paid tiers only launch after pilot stores confirm willingness to pay.

  • Pricing plans and billing (currently no payment functionality)
  • Public 'Verified by Valuon' widget for merchant websites
  • Paid verification API for insurers and resale marketplaces
  • Onboarding checklist for new stores in the dashboard
Paid tier launches only after written merchant confirmation, not at the same time as the pilot.
Год 2
A + B → C decision

Stage 5: geographic and legal expansion

From one country and one role to multiple jurisdictions. Each new market launches only after measurable success in the previous one.

  • Role A pilot in Estonia (5–10 merchants) — Valuon as their only receipt system
  • Parallel small-scale Role A pilot in the US
  • First Role B integration: TSE/cash register API connector (Germany)
  • Legal consultation: can an imported receipt be called 'official'
  • Choose primary legal jurisdiction (Estonia)
  • Expand Role A to Ireland/Finland on Estonia success
  • Expand Role B to France (cash register certification from Sep 2026)
  • Role C decision — only under confirmed demand, not before 2028
Key metric: D30 retention ≥ 25%. If retention is low, consider an embeddable widget instead of a standalone app.
